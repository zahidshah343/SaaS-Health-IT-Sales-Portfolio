## About This Portfolio

This repository contains work samples from a six month engagement with ReadySetConnect, a US based HIPAA compliant EMR and Practice Management SaaS platform serving therapy practices in OT, ABA, and SLP.

The documents here demonstrate capabilities in sales infrastructure design, revenue cycle management, go to market strategy, and remote team leadership for US healthcare clients.

---

## What Is Included

### 1. SDR Workflow Overview
A complete sales development representative workflow covering lead reception, qualification criteria, outreach cadence, live transfer protocols, and daily targets. Designed for efficient lead handling and high conversion to demos.

### 2. Sales Compensation Plan and Call Criteria
A milestone based compensation model defining qualified conversation criteria, demo booked and completed fees, closed sale commissions, and reporting standards. This plan formed the basis of the formal consultant agreement.

### 3. Growth Plan for ZeeMed and ReadySetConnect
A quarterly revenue roadmap covering software subscription revenue, RCM revenue splits, combined projections through Q2 2027, and a US versus Pakistan cost comparison showing approximately 85 percent savings.

### 4. CPT and Billing Configuration Review
A technical review of CPT code availability, service to CPT mappings, OT evaluation code configuration, authorization considerations, and recommended implementation sequence for the RSC billing platform.

### 5. Education and CEU Strategy
A product led growth strategy leveraging the platform built in CEU library as a lead magnet, upsell pathway, and retention tool. Includes messaging anchors and expected impact on lead generation and conversion.

### 6. Sales Commission Report
A performance summary covering 222 qualified sales conversations, 19 demos booked, and 12 demos completed over the engagement period.
